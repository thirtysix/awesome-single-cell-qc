# Preprocessing concepts: how QC choices reach normalisation, feature selection, integration and clustering

Part of the adjacent concept map. This chapter is lighter than the core chapters: one node per step, the standard tools and benchmarks for it, and the evidence for how a QC decision changes that step's result. Each concept record carries these as `qc_links`. The downstream steps (DE, perturbation, trajectories, multimodal) are in the [downstream chapter](22-concepts-downstream.md).

## How QC choices propagate

Normalisation, feature selection, integration and clustering run on whatever QC hands them, and none of them can tell an artefact from a cell. The evidence shows four routes.

- **Depth left in becomes structure.** Under log-CPM, the fraction of zeros in each cell, which falls as total UMIs rise, drives the first principal component in most datasets [scry deviance feature selection and GLM-PCA](../records.md#townes-2019); technical cell-to-cell variation in zeros can be mistaken for biology (Hicks 2018, [hicks-2018](../records.md#hicks-2018)). Lenient cell calling keeps more low-count cells, so the normalisation and gene selection that follow must handle depth on counts, not on CPM ([ahlmann-eltze-2023](../records.md#ahlmann-eltze-2023), [Analytic Pearson residuals](../records.md#lause-2021)).
- **Covariates regressed out instead of handled at QC make things worse.** In pipeComp, regressing out mito proportion or detected genes at the scaling step nearly always increased correlation with those covariates and lowered clustering accuracy [pipeComp](../records.md#pipecomp). Damaged cells are better handled at QC ([MAD-based adaptive QC thresholds](../records.md#mad-adaptive-qc), [DropletQC](../records.md#dropletqc)) than corrected afterwards.
- **Artefactual cells become clusters and labels.** Ambient RNA defined some previously annotated neuronal types in brain nuclei, and the reported immature oligodendrocytes were glia contaminated with ambient RNA [caglayan-2022](../records.md#caglayan-2022). Multiplets are several percent of transcriptomes and confound downstream analysis [Scrublet](../records.md#scrublet); distribution-based QC filters do not catch them [pipeComp](../records.md#pipecomp), so doublet detection ([scDblFinder](../records.md#scdblfinder)) has to happen before clustering.
- **Strict global thresholds remove biology before anything downstream sees it.** Stringent whole-population filtering discarded subpopulations with extreme but healthy properties and did not improve clustering [pipeComp](../records.md#pipecomp); a 5% mito cut misclassified cells in 13 of 44 human tissues [osorio-cai-2021](../records.md#osorio-cai-2021). Integration then aligns batches whose composition QC has already changed.

## Normalisation

[Normalisation and variance-stabilising transformation](../records.md#concept-normalisation). Size factors correct for each cell's capture and sequencing depth; a transformation then makes variance comparable across expression levels. The best independent evidence is [ahlmann-eltze-2023](../records.md#ahlmann-eltze-2023): of 22 transformations, the shifted logarithm of size-factor-scaled counts followed by PCA did as well as or better than the rest, Pearson residuals did about as well, and the shifted log on CPM (1e6) did poorly. pipeComp rated [sctransform](../records.md#sctransform) best for separating subpopulations and removing library-size and detection-rate effects, with most other methods, [scran pooled size factors](../records.md#scran-pooling) included, fair [pipeComp](../records.md#pipecomp). [Analytic Pearson residuals](../records.md#lause-2021) showed that sctransform's model reduces to an analytic Pearson residual and that UMI data are close to Poisson. A smaller benchmark of six methods [ge-2025](../records.md#ge-2025) put Dino first for 10x data and sctransform first for full-length protocols; it weighs less than the two above. Scaling every cell to the same total removes real differences in transcriptome size between cell types, often several-fold, so cross-type DE after CP10K reports size as expression [ReDeconv and CLTS normalisation](../records.md#redeconv).

QC links. Depth and zeros (above). Do not regress out mito fraction as a substitute for QC. Per-cell normalisation does not remove batch: C1 technical replicates of the same iPSC lines differed substantially (Tung 2017, [tung-2017](../records.md#tung-2017)). Reviews: Vallejos 2017 (PMID 28504683), Luecken and Theis 2019 [luecken-theis-2019](../records.md#luecken-theis-2019), Heumos 2023 [heumos-2023](../records.md#heumos-2023).

## Feature selection

[Feature selection](../records.md#concept-feature-selection). Picking 2,000 to 4,000 genes whose variation exceeds technical expectation sets the PCA, the graph and integration. Deviance on raw counts gave the best gene ranking in pipeComp, with gains up to about 4,000 genes [scry deviance feature selection and GLM-PCA](../records.md#townes-2019). For integration and query mapping, [zappia-2025](../records.md#zappia-2025) found Seurat-VST highly variable genes top-ranked across 24 methods and about 2,000 features sufficient. [luecken-2022-scib](../records.md#luecken-2022-scib) found HVG selection improved 74% of integration comparisons.

QC links. Selecting HVGs on log-CPM of cells with very different depths produces false variability [scry deviance feature selection and GLM-PCA](../records.md#townes-2019). HVG restriction lowered cell-cycle and trajectory conservation relative to the full gene set [luecken-2022-scib](../records.md#luecken-2022-scib), so whether cycle and stress programmes ([scLVM](../records.md#sclvm), [van-den-brink-2017](../records.md#van-den-brink-2017)) survive depends on the gene list. Batch-aware scanpy HVG variants gave no consistent advantage [zappia-2025](../records.md#zappia-2025). Reviews: Yang 2021 (PMID 34847932), Yip 2019 (PMID 29481632).

## Integration and batch correction

[Data integration and batch correction](../records.md#concept-integration). Integration removes whatever is declared as batch while trying to keep identities and states. Two independent benchmarks disagree in a useful way. On simpler tasks, [tran-2020](../records.md#tran-2020) recommended [Harmony](../records.md#harmony), LIGER and [Seurat anchor integration](../records.md#seurat-integration), Harmony first for speed. On atlas tasks, [luecken-2022-scib](../records.md#luecken-2022-scib) ranked scANVI, [Scanorama](../records.md#scanorama), scVI ([scvi-tools](../records.md#scvi-tools)) and scGen highest; [MNN correction and fastMNN](../records.md#fastmnn) and Harmony were among the best on the immune task, and [BBKNN](../records.md#bbknn) corrects only the graph. Scaling pushed every method toward batch removal (79% of comparisons) at the cost of bio-conservation (72%).

QC links. The batch key chosen at QC (lane, plate, donor, study) defines what is removed. Ambient load depends on preparation: neuronal ambient RNA was in every glial type unless glia and neurons were separated before sequencing [caglayan-2022](../records.md#caglayan-2022), so ambient differs between batches and is best corrected per sample before integration ([CellBender remove-background](../records.md#cellbender), [DecontX](../records.md#decontx), [SoupX](../records.md#soupx)); this order is advice, not a tested result. Methods returning corrected expression (Scanorama gene output, MNN) conserved cell-cycle variance best [luecken-2022-scib](../records.md#luecken-2022-scib). A global threshold that removes a cell type in some tissues only [osorio-cai-2021](../records.md#osorio-cai-2021) leaves batches with different composition, the harder "non-identical cell types" case in [tran-2020](../records.md#tran-2020). Review: Argelaguet 2021 (PMID 33941931).

## Clustering and annotation

[Clustering and cell-type annotation](../records.md#concept-clustering-annotation). Graph clustering, usually [Leiden community detection](../records.md#leiden) on a kNN graph, groups cells; annotation names them by markers, by classifiers trained on references ([CellTypist](../records.md#celltypist), [SingleR](../records.md#singler)) or by mapping to an atlas ([Azimuth reference mapping](../records.md#azimuth), scANVI in [scvi-tools](../records.md#scvi-tools)). In [abdelaal-2019](../records.md#abdelaal-2019), a linear SVM was the best of 22 classifiers, SingleR was among the top five at deep annotation but slowest, and accuracy fell for overlapping classes and deep annotation levels. SC3 and Seurat led an earlier clustering benchmark (Duò 2018, PMID 30271584). CellTypist and Azimuth have no independent peer-reviewed benchmark yet.

QC links. Ambient RNA created and masked cell types [caglayan-2022](../records.md#caglayan-2022). Doublets confound clusters and markers, and removing them improved DE gene detection [DoubletFinder](../records.md#doubletfinder). Strict thresholds remove subpopulations [pipeComp](../records.md#pipecomp). Dissociation induced immediate-early and heat-shock genes in subpopulations [van-den-brink-2017](../records.md#van-den-brink-2017), and accounting for cell cycle revealed otherwise hidden T-helper subpopulations [scLVM](../records.md#sclvm). Reference annotators label every cell with a reference type, so damaged or doublet barcodes left in receive a confident-looking label unless a rejection option or mapping score is checked. Markers found by DE between clusters on the same cells that defined them have artificially low p-values (double dipping) [TN test](../records.md#tn-test); [ClusterDE](../records.md#clusterde) repeats the clustering and DE on a matched one-population synthetic null to remove markers that over-clustering created. Distances and cluster sizes read off 2D UMAP or t-SNE are distorted [chari-pachter-2023](../records.md#chari-pachter-2023), so QC calls are better made in PCA space or on the metrics themselves. Reviews: Kiselev 2019 (PMID 30617341), Clarke 2021 (PMID 34031612).
