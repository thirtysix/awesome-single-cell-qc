# Doublets, cell-cycle and stress signatures, batch and lane effects

Scope: every RNA, ATAC and multiome doublet detector with real use, how independent benchmarks rank them, cell-cycle assignment and removal, dissociation and handling stress, and technical lane, plate and batch effects treated as QC questions (detection and diagnosis, not integration). The calling and damage review already covers scDblFinder, the Xi and Li and singletCode results, and the first dissociation-stress papers ([scDblFinder](../records.md#scdblfinder), [xi-li-2021](../records.md#xi-li-2021), [singletcode-2024](../records.md#singletcode-2024), [van-den-brink-2017](../records.md#van-den-brink-2017), [oflanagan-2019](../records.md#oflanagan-2019)); this chapter adds the rest and does not repeat their numbers. Integration methods themselves are in the concept map ([Harmony](../records.md#harmony)).

## Which of these three removes cells, and how much it costs

Of the three topics, only doublet calling routinely deletes barcodes. Cell-cycle and stress handling usually change values rather than drop cells, and batch diagnostics delete nothing, but each can remove biology if used as a correction without a design that separates it from the effect of interest.

**Doublets.** Detectors are run at a removal rate, and the rate is usually the vendor rule of about 0.8% per 1,000 cells recovered [Expected doublet rate from loading](../records.md#expected-doublet-rate). In a hashed 20,000-cell lane that is about 16% of barcodes. Against hashing truth, the 10x rule underestimated multiplets, yet only 164 of 7,186 hashing multiplets (2.3%) were found by all eight tools tested, and the tools disagreed on which clusters held doublets: hashing put about 43% of its multiplets in one cluster where the tools found very few (PMID 41166377). So an RNA detector run at the expected rate removes a large set of barcodes that overlaps the true doublets only in part. More rounds of removal raise recall (two DoubletFinder rounds: recall +50%) by removing more barcodes [Multi-round doublet removal](../records.md#multi-round-doublet-removal). In the Demuxafy comparison, 0.4% to 78.8% of droplets were wrongly classified by the demultiplexers and detectors depending on method and pool size, and two detectors (DoubletDecon and DoubletFinder) misclassified noticeably more singlets than the rest [Demuxafy](../records.md#demuxafy), [DoubletDecon](../records.md#doubletdecon), [DoubletFinder](../records.md#doubletfinder).

**Cell cycle.** Regressing out phase scores is a default tutorial step [Regressing out cell-cycle scores](../records.md#cell-cycle-regression). It removes no cells but it removes every expression change correlated with the scores, which in a drug screen includes the arrest a drug causes [cell-cycle-as-readout](../records.md#cell-cycle-as-readout).

**Stress.** Handling time alone changed 1,185 genes in PBMC after 2 h at room temperature [massoni-badosa-2020](../records.md#massoni-badosa-2020); filtering the stressed cells, as Gruffi does in organoids [Gruffi](../records.md#gruffi), removes them outright, and dissociation choice can decide which disease-state cells survive at all [dostalova-2026](../records.md#dostalova-2026).

**Keeping them safely** means using orthogonal truth where it exists (tags, genotypes, receptor chains, read counts per locus in ATAC), choosing detectors by independent benchmark rather than by default, treating phase and stress as measured covariates that are reported per condition, and designing lanes and plates so that technical units are never confounded with condition.

## Standard practice

- **Doublets.** Python pipelines (scanpy) use [Scrublet](../records.md#scrublet); the scverse and OSCA guides use [scDblFinder](../records.md#scdblfinder); Seurat users commonly use [DoubletFinder](../records.md#doubletfinder). All are run per capture lane at an expected rate [Expected doublet rate from loading](../records.md#expected-doublet-rate). Hashing and genotype pooling remove cross-sample doublets ([Cell Hashing](../records.md#cell-hashing), [Demuxafy](../records.md#demuxafy)). For scATAC, the ArchR workflow scores doublets by projecting synthetic doublets [ArchR](../records.md#archr).
- **Cell cycle.** Seurat `CellCycleScoring` and scanpy `score_genes_cell_cycle` score the Tirosh S and G2/M lists [Seurat CellCycleScoring / scanpy score_genes_cell_cycle](../records.md#seurat-cell-cycle-scoring); OSCA uses scran's [cyclone](../records.md#cyclone). Removal is by regressing the scores, or only their difference to keep cycling and non-cycling cells apart [Regressing out cell-cycle scores](../records.md#cell-cycle-regression).
- **Stress.** Most pipelines do nothing by default. Common practice is to score a dissociation gene list and inspect it ([van-den-brink-2017](../records.md#van-den-brink-2017)); at the bench, cold protease [Cold-active protease dissociation](../records.md#cold-protease-dissociation) or transcription inhibitors [Transcription and translation inhibitors during dissociation](../records.md#transcription-inhibitor-dissociation), and cold storage of blood before processing [massoni-badosa-2020](../records.md#massoni-badosa-2020).
- **Batch, lane and plate.** Look at QC metrics per lane and plate and at each cluster's batch composition before integrating [Per-batch QC distributions and per-cluster batch composition](../records.md#batch-composition-diagnostic), [scRNABatchQC](../records.md#scrnabatchqc). kBET and LISI are the usual numerical diagnostics and are among the scIB integration metrics [kBET](../records.md#kbet), [LISI](../records.md#lisi). When samples share a sequencing lane on patterned flow cells, DropletUtils `swappedDrops` removes swapped molecules [swappedDrops (DropletUtils) barcode-swapping removal](../records.md#swappeddrops).

## Benchmarked state of the art

**RNA doublet detectors.** Four independent studies score the same tools; together they say that the leading group is DoubletFinder, Scrublet, scds, Solo, DoubletDetection and scDblFinder, and that the order within it depends on data and metric.

- Xi and Li (16 real datasets with annotated doublets, 112 simulated): DoubletFinder had the highest mean AUPRC (0.537) and Solo the highest mean AUROC; Solo and hybrid were the only methods that beat the library-size and gene-count baselines on every dataset, and doubletCells the only one that did not beat them on most. DoubletDetection and DoubletFinder most reliably restored the correct number of clusters; cxds was fastest and most resource-efficient; hybrid improved on both its components at little cost [DoubletFinder](../records.md#doubletfinder), [Solo](../records.md#solo), [scds](../records.md#scds), [DoubletDetection](../records.md#doubletdetection), [doubletCells / computeDoubletDensity](../records.md#doubletcells). A best mean AUPRC near 0.5 means that, averaged over thresholds, about half of the barcodes ranked as doublets were not annotated doublets.
- Demuxafy (pooled PBMC and in silico pools of 2-128 donors): scds, scDblFinder and Scrublet were the top detectors by MCC and balanced accuracy; scDblFinder and Scrublet classified the most doublets correctly up to 16 donors and Solo did better than scds above that; DoubletDetection had the lowest share of wrong droplets (about 1-3%); Solo took a median 13 h per pool. Scrublet and scDblFinder were the most affected by added ambient RNA [Demuxafy](../records.md#demuxafy), [Scrublet](../records.md#scrublet).
- Ttoouli and Hoffmann (eight tools tuned per dataset against cell hashing): Scrublet most accurate by AUROC; after removal, DoubletDetection, scDblFinder and DoubletFinder gave the best clustering, ahead of removing the hashing multiplets alone [Scrublet](../records.md#scrublet), [DoubletDetection](../records.md#doubletdetection).
- singletCode (lineage-barcoded singlets): weak performance for all four detectors tested ([singletcode-2024](../records.md#singletcode-2024)); its ensemble test is the useful addition here.

**Ensembles do not reliably beat their parts.** Chord's own paper reports higher accuracy than its components, but Demuxafy's intersection of scds and DoubletFinder beat Chord in pools of 8 or more donors, and on singletCode data neither hybrid nor Chord always beat their components [Chord](../records.md#chord). Combining a detector with tags or genotypes helps more than combining detectors: Demuxafy's recommended combinations pair one or two detectors with demultiplexing [Demuxafy](../records.md#demuxafy).

**Cell-cycle assignment.** One independent comparison exists. Guo and Chen ran Tricycle, Seurat scoring, Revelio and CS1CC on four small plate-based datasets with phases labelled by FUCCI or DNA staining. No method was consistently best; Tricycle was accurate on mouse and less so on human, matching its mouse reference; Seurat and Revelio assign every cell to a phase whether or not it is cycling; adding cells of known phase to the query improved every method [guo-chen-2024](../records.md#guo-chen-2024), [tricycle](../records.md#tricycle), [Seurat CellCycleScoring / scanpy score_genes_cell_cycle](../records.md#seurat-cell-cycle-scoring), [Revelio](../records.md#revelio). Cyclone was not tested; the CycleMix authors report that both Seurat and cyclone perform poorly on droplet data and that Seurat labels mature neurons as cycling, a competitor comparison [CycleMix](../records.md#cyclemix), [cyclone](../records.md#cyclone).

**ATAC and multiome doublets, stress correction, batch metrics.** No independent benchmark was found for any of these. Each tool below was evaluated only by its authors or by a competitor.

## Published but not independently benchmarked

**RNA doublets.** [vaeda](../records.md#vaeda) (from the scds group) reports competitive scores on the 16 Xi and Li datasets and the best result among Python tools. [SoCube](../records.md#socube), [RADO](../records.md#rado) (preprint) and [Xoublet](../records.md#xoublet) are newer detectors; Xoublet's own paper reports it is faster and lighter than Scrublet on 2.5 million cells but less discriminative on two of three benchmarks. [ImageDoubler](../records.md#imagedoubler) uses capture-site images on Fluidigm C1, up to 93.87% detection, and does not apply to droplets. [Multi-round doublet removal](../records.md#multi-round-doublet-removal) is a strategy, not a tool.

**Orthogonal doublet signals.** Receptor chains and surface proteins mark doublets that expression cannot: over half of T cells with several TCR chains were doublets in standard and superloaded runs [multi-tcr-doublets](../records.md#multi-tcr-doublets), and Double-jeopardy trains on such droplets to find more [Double-jeopardy](../records.md#double-jeopardy). In hashed data, a doublet can lose one of its labels and pass as a singlet; Nakaki and Sharpe model these partial-stealth multiplets and find them in real hashed and genotype-pooled data [stealth-multiplets](../records.md#stealth-multiplets).

**ATAC and multiome doublets.** [AMULET](../records.md#amulet) counts loci with more than two reads, which a diploid nucleus cannot produce, so it sees homotypic multiplets; on simulated PBMC multiplets its recall was 0.80-0.90 against 0.23-0.25 for ArchR, but on shallower islet data (below about 25K median reads) it fell to 0.35-0.37 and ArchR was better on one sample. scDblFinder's ATAC mode aggregates correlated peaks; its authors found AMULET good on one of three datasets and offer a combination of both ([scDblFinder](../records.md#scdblfinder)). [scIBD](../records.md#scibd) targets heterotypic ATAC doublets. For multiome, [COMPOSITE](../records.md#composite) (17 DOGMA-seq datasets with hashing truth, 280,123 droplets), [OmniDoublet](../records.md#omnidoublet) and [SEBULA](../records.md#sebula) (FDR-controlled, no synthetic doublets) combine modalities; each reports wins over the others' predecessors.

**Continuous cell-cycle position and removal.** [peco](../records.md#peco) (five genes, within 14% of the cycle in iPSCs), [Cyclum](../records.md#cyclum), [reCAT](../records.md#recat), [CCPE](../records.md#ccpe), [DeepCycle](../records.md#deepcycle) and [VeloCycle](../records.md#velocycle) (phase and speed from RNA velocity) give a position rather than a label. [ccAFv2](../records.md#ccafv2) (preprint) adds a neural G0 state and leaves uncertain cells unclassified; [CycleMix](../records.md#cyclemix) can call cells non-cycling. For removal, [ccRemover](../records.md#ccremover) and [scLVM](../records.md#sclvm) remove only cell-cycle components, and [CycleVI](../records.md#cyclevi) separates them into a latent subspace and reports better preservation of drug-response signal than score regression. ccRemover, scLVM and reCAT are no longer maintained.

**Stress.** Dissociation stress is conserved across tissues and present in published atlases [machado-2021](../records.md#machado-2021), strongest in brain myeloid cells, where enzymatic digestion creates an ex vivo activated microglia cluster that an inhibitor cocktail prevents [Transcription and translation inhibitors during dissociation](../records.md#transcription-inhibitor-dissociation); 37 C enzymatic brain dissociation also changed neuronal and glial profiles against 4 C mechanical [mattei-2020](../records.md#mattei-2020). Cold handling works for blood too: no global artefact after up to 48 h at 4 C [massoni-badosa-2020](../records.md#massoni-badosa-2020). Computational rescue is partial: a sampling-time score flagged affected cells with AUC 0.888 and its regression helped mainly for delays under 8 h [massoni-badosa-2020](../records.md#massoni-badosa-2020); tissue-specific stress panels removed 60-64% of the artefact against about 95% for the gentler protocol [Stress-altered transcription gene panels](../records.md#sat-gene-panels); Gruffi removes stressed organoid cells [Gruffi](../records.md#gruffi). The bench beats the computer.

**Batch and lane diagnostics.** [CellMixS](../records.md#cellmixs) gives a per-cell mixing score that separates local batch bias from unbalanced composition. [GTE (group technical effects) per-gene batch metric](../records.md#gte-batch-genes) scores batch per gene and reports that as few as three highly batch-sensitive genes create a substantial batch effect, which suggests checking and excluding such genes before integrating. Silhouette-based batch scores are unreliable for this purpose [rautenstrauch-2026](../records.md#rautenstrauch-2026). Index hopping is a lane-level artefact: a hopping probability of only 0.003-0.009 left more than 8% phantom molecules in over a quarter of samples and up to 85% in low-complexity samples, creating phantom cells [PhantomPurgeR](../records.md#phantompurger), [swappedDrops (DropletUtils) barcode-swapping removal](../records.md#swappeddrops).

## Artefact or biology: how to tell

**Doublet or transitional state.** A cluster that expresses two lineages' markers may be doublets or a real intermediate. Signals that separate them: doublets have about twice the UMIs of their parents (but so do large or G2/M cells), are enriched for cross-sample tags in hashed data, carry two receptor chains in lymphocytes [multi-tcr-doublets](../records.md#multi-tcr-doublets), and in ATAC show loci with more than two reads [AMULET](../records.md#amulet). DoubletDecon's rescue step returns such clusters when they carry unique genes [DoubletDecon](../records.md#doubletdecon); DoubletFinder's authors showed it left a validated hybrid kidney cell type alone [DoubletFinder](../records.md#doubletfinder).

**Homotypic doublets.** Two cells of the same type look like one large cell. Expression-based detectors largely miss them ([singletcode-2024](../records.md#singletcode-2024)); only tags, genotypes, receptor chains or ATAC read counts can see them. In a single-line screen this is most doublets.

**Cycling or treated.** G2/M cells have more RNA and can be mistaken for doublets by UMI caps. Phase fractions move with treatment, so a phase shift between conditions is a result, not a nuisance [cell-cycle-as-readout](../records.md#cell-cycle-as-readout). Check phase calls with two callers, since no caller is consistently best [guo-chen-2024](../records.md#guo-chen-2024).

**Handled or responding.** The dissociation and handling signature (FOS, JUN, ATF3, heat-shock genes, NFKBIA) overlaps genuine stress responses to injury [machado-2021](../records.md#machado-2021) and, plausibly, to drugs. Only a control processed identically, in the same lane and at the same time, separates them [handling-vs-drug-stress](../records.md#handling-vs-drug-stress).

**Batch or biology.** A cluster made of one lane is an artefact only if lanes do not differ in condition. With plates confounded with groups, cell-level DE loses type I error control; summing per plate restores it but needs several plates per group [lun-marioni-2017](../records.md#lun-marioni-2017). Technical replicates of the same lines already vary substantially [tung-2017](../records.md#tung-2017), and confounded designs have produced spurious findings in published studies [hicks-2018](../records.md#hicks-2018). kBET and LISI cannot tell a batch effect from batch-specific cell types without labels [kBET](../records.md#kbet), [LISI](../records.md#lisi).

## Hypotheses and open questions

- [cell-cycle-as-readout](../records.md#cell-cycle-as-readout): drugs that arrest cells change phase fractions; global regression removes that response. Report phase per condition and remove cell-cycle variation within condition or by disentangling. CycleVI's drug-response result is self-reported.
- [handling-vs-drug-stress](../records.md#handling-vs-drug-stress): separate handling stress from drug-induced stress with vehicle wells handled in the same lane and time. Untested in screens.
- Open: no independent benchmark of ATAC or multiome doublet callers, of stress-signature correction, or of batch-diagnostic metrics was found. Cell-cycle callers have one small independent comparison on plate-based cycling cells; none on droplet data, non-cycling cells or drug-treated cells. RNA detectors have not been benchmarked on drug-treated cells, where RNA content and state change with dose.

## Recommendations for low-cell multiplexed drug screens

Ranked by expected gain per unit of effort. Gains are from the cited settings; none was measured in a drug screen.

1. **Let tags do the doublet work; use RNA detectors only for what tags cannot see.** Remove cross-sample doublets by hashing or genotype first ([Cell Hashing](../records.md#cell-hashing), [Demuxafy](../records.md#demuxafy)), then run one leading detector per lane ([Scrublet](../records.md#scrublet), [scds](../records.md#scds), [scDblFinder](../records.md#scdblfinder) or [DoubletFinder](../records.md#doubletfinder)) where cell types are mixed. *Gain:* demultiplexing plus a detector beat detectors or ensembles alone in Demuxafy [Demuxafy](../records.md#demuxafy), [Chord](../records.md#chord). *Data:* tag counts, filtered matrix.
2. **Do not delete at the vendor rate blindly.** In a single cell line most within-sample doublets are homotypic and invisible to RNA scores [singletcode-2024](../records.md#singletcode-2024). *Gain:* avoids removing about 16% of a superloaded lane on a score that overlaps poorly with true doublets [Expected doublet rate from loading](../records.md#expected-doublet-rate). *Data:* filtered matrix.
3. **Design lanes and plates as blocks.** Put every condition, including vehicle, in every lane or plate, so lane and plate effects are estimable and DE can be done per lane or plate in pseudobulk ([lun-marioni-2017](../records.md#lun-marioni-2017), [Pseudobulk differential expression](../records.md#pseudobulk-de), [scattered-vehicle-controls](../records.md#scattered-vehicle-controls), [Independent replicate wells or cultures per condition](../records.md#replicate-wells)). *Gain:* restores type I error control under plate effects [lun-marioni-2017](../records.md#lun-marioni-2017). *Data:* replicates.
4. **Diagnose before integrating.** QC metrics per lane, cluster composition per lane, kBET or LISI on vehicle cells only (where biology should match) [Per-batch QC distributions and per-cluster batch composition](../records.md#batch-composition-diagnostic), [kBET](../records.md#kbet), [LISI](../records.md#lisi); check for a few highly batch-sensitive genes [GTE (group technical effects) per-gene batch metric](../records.md#gte-batch-genes). If several libraries share a sequencing lane on a patterned flow cell, run [swappedDrops (DropletUtils) barcode-swapping removal](../records.md#swappeddrops). *Gain:* phantom molecules up to 85% in low-complexity samples [PhantomPurgeR](../records.md#phantompurger). *Data:* filtered matrix; molecule info or BAM for swapping.
5. **Keep cell cycle as a readout.** Assign phase with two callers (for example [tricycle](../records.md#tricycle) and [Seurat CellCycleScoring / scanpy score_genes_cell_cycle](../records.md#seurat-cell-cycle-scoring), or [CycleMix](../records.md#cyclemix) where non-cycling cells are present), report phase fractions per drug and dose, and avoid global score regression before DE [cell-cycle-as-readout](../records.md#cell-cycle-as-readout), [CycleVI](../records.md#cyclevi). *Gain:* keeps arrest effects that regression removes; none measured. *Data:* filtered matrix.
6. **Prevent handling stress at the bench and measure the rest against vehicle.** Fix or process all wells at the same time and temperature; for tissue, cold dissociation or inhibitors ([Cold-active protease dissociation](../records.md#cold-protease-dissociation), [Transcription and translation inhibitors during dissociation](../records.md#transcription-inhibitor-dissociation)); for blood, 4 C until processing [massoni-badosa-2020](../records.md#massoni-badosa-2020); fixation designs are in the design review ([Methanol fixation](../records.md#methanol-fixation), [10x Chromium Flex](../records.md#10x-flex)). Score the stress signature per condition and read only the excess over vehicle as response [handling-vs-drug-stress](../records.md#handling-vs-drug-stress). *Gain:* about 95% less artefact from the gentler protocol against 60-64% from computational panels [Stress-altered transcription gene panels](../records.md#sat-gene-panels). *Data:* vehicle controls per lane.
