# Downstream concepts: how QC choices reach DE, perturbation, trajectories and multimodal results

Part of the adjacent concept map. This chapter is lighter than the core chapters: one node per downstream step, the standard tools for it, and above all the evidence for how a QC or preprocessing decision changes that step's result. Each concept record carries these as `qc_links`. Detailed DE and composition statistics are in the low-cell review ([Pseudobulk differential expression](../records.md#pseudobulk-de), [muscat](../records.md#muscat), [squair-2021](../records.md#squair-2021)); the multimodal QC tools themselves are core records ([dsb](../records.md#dsb)).

## How QC choices propagate

QC decides which barcodes become cells and what is counted in them. Every downstream step then treats that set as the experiment. Four routes recur in the evidence:

- **Losses that differ by condition become results.** A drug or a disease state can move exactly the metrics QC filters on. Malignant cells carry higher mitochondrial fractions without more dissociation stress, and high mito tracks drug resistance [mito-filter-resistant-cells](../records.md#mito-filter-resistant-cells); dying cells under navitoclax left no robust transcriptional signature among the cells captured [survivor-bias](../records.md#survivor-bias). A fixed cut then changes DE, abundance and perturbation estimates at once. The audit for this is in [condition-aware-qc-audit](../records.md#condition-aware-qc-audit).
- **Artefactual cells become biology.** Heterotypic doublets made spurious branches in Slingshot trajectories and degraded DE gene recovery until they were removed (Xi and Li 2021, [xi-li-2021](../records.md#xi-li-2021)).
- **Background becomes signal.** Ambient RNA is sample-specific, so uncorrected DE between patients and controls called ambient transcripts as disease genes [FastCAR](../records.md#fastcar); ambient gRNAs create false guide assignments in Perturb-seq [CLEANSER](../records.md#cleanser); free antibody makes ADT background [dsb](../records.md#dsb).
- **The quantifier sets the input.** RNA velocity reads spliced and unspliced counts that differ substantially between quantification tools [soneson-2021-velocity-preprocessing](../records.md#soneson-2021-velocity-preprocessing), and only three of 22 velocity methods were robust to the choice (PMID 42509564).

## Differential expression and abundance

[Differential expression and differential abundance between conditions](../records.md#concept-de-pseudobulk). Testing between conditions treats the replicate as the unit. Against matched bulk data, all six best methods were pseudobulk and cell-level tests called hundreds of false DE genes [squair-2021](../records.md#squair-2021); [junttila-2022](../records.md#junttila-2022) agrees on multi-subject designs, and [pseudobulk-vs-mixed-debate](../records.md#pseudobulk-vs-mixed-debate) covers the mixed-model side. Standard tools: [muscat](../records.md#muscat), [dreamlet](../records.md#dreamlet), [NEBULA](../records.md#nebula), [scVI differential expression](../records.md#scvi-de) and [MrVI](../records.md#mrvi) for DE; [propeller (speckle) and Scanpro](../records.md#propeller), [scCODA](../records.md#sccoda), [sccomp](../records.md#sccomp) and [Milo](../records.md#milo) for abundance.

QC links. Ambient RNA must be corrected per sample before cross-condition DE (FastCAR, [FastCAR](../records.md#fastcar); CellBender reports the same risk, [CellBender remove-background](../records.md#cellbender)). Doublet removal improved DE gene recovery in simulations [xi-li-2021](../records.md#xi-li-2021). QC losses lower cells per replicate, and samples under `min_cells` drop out of [muscat](../records.md#muscat); [dreamlet](../records.md#dreamlet) down-weights them instead. Abundance tests are the most direct casualty of condition-specific filtering, since a cell type removed by a mito cut in one arm reads as depletion.

**Milo.** [Milo](../records.md#milo) tests abundance on overlapping kNN neighbourhoods rather than clusters. In an independent benchmark of six DA methods, Milo and Meld were the two most robust, and Milo was best across batch-effect magnitudes with the fewest hyperparameters (Yi 2024, PMID 38172966). miloDE extends the approach to cluster-free DE (PMID 39026254).

## Perturbation modelling

[Perturbation response estimation and prediction](../records.md#concept-perturbation-models). Two tasks: estimating the effect of each perturbation against controls, and predicting unseen perturbations, doses or combinations (review, PMID 34139164). For estimation, [Mixscape](../records.md#mixscape) classifies cells that escaped perturbation, [TRADE](../records.md#trade) estimates transcriptome-wide impact when single genes are underpowered, and [pertpy](../records.md#pertpy) bundles these and other methods for scverse. For prediction, [CPA and chemCPA](../records.md#cpa) models dose and combinations, but none of five foundation models nor GEARS or CPA beat additive or linear baselines on held-out genetic perturbations [ahlmann-eltze-2025](../records.md#ahlmann-eltze-2025). [unbalanced-ot-cell-loss](../records.md#unbalanced-ot-cell-loss) proposes modelling the cells a drug removes rather than ignoring them.

QC links. Ambient gRNAs produce false-positive guide assignments, and the filter chosen changed DE results (CLEANSER, [CLEANSER](../records.md#cleanser)). Cells that die are absent from the response estimate [survivor-bias](../records.md#survivor-bias), while the count of cells recovered per hashed condition is itself a viability readout [Cell recovery per condition as a viability readout](../records.md#cell-recovery-viability). A fixed mito cut can remove resistant cells preferentially [mito-filter-resistant-cells](../records.md#mito-filter-resistant-cells). Comparing each cell with its nearest controls reduced replicate, cell-cycle and stress variation in Mixscape signatures [Mixscape](../records.md#mixscape).

## Trajectories and RNA velocity

[Trajectory inference, pseudotime and RNA velocity](../records.md#concept-trajectories). Pseudotime methods order cells along a process; RNA velocity infers its direction from splicing kinetics; fate-mapping methods combine the two (review, PMID 31249007; velocity pitfalls, PMID 34435732). Across 45 methods, the best choice depended mostly on dataset size and topology [saelens-2019](../records.md#saelens-2019); in its preprint and in the authors' results data [Slingshot](../records.md#slingshot) was the most accurate overall. [velocyto](../records.md#velocyto) introduced velocity, [scVelo](../records.md#scvelo) is the scverse default, and [CellRank](../records.md#cellrank) turns velocity or pseudotime into fate probabilities. Two independent 2026 benchmarks (30 and 15 methods) found no single best velocity method; both recommended scVelo's stochastic mode among their choices (PMIDs 42509564, 41916302).

QC links. Doublets create spurious branches [xi-li-2021](../records.md#xi-li-2021). Cell-cycle variation hid stages of naive T to Th2 differentiation until it was modelled [scLVM](../records.md#sclvm); see the cell-cycle tools in the core chapters. Velocity is only as good as the spliced and unspliced counts, and they differ by quantification pipeline [soneson-2021-velocity-preprocessing](../records.md#soneson-2021-velocity-preprocessing).

## Multimodal data

[Multimodal single-cell data](../records.md#concept-multimodal). CITE-seq adds antibody-derived protein counts and multiome adds chromatin accessibility, each with its own noise (review, PMID 32929225). [Seurat weighted nearest neighbours](../records.md#seurat-wnn) weights each modality per cell for a joint graph; [totalVI](../records.md#totalvi) models RNA and protein with an explicit protein background. In an independent benchmark of 47 datasets, totalVI was among the best for protein prediction and for horizontal and mosaic integration, and Seurat led vertical integration with MOJITOO and scAI (Hu 2024, PMID 39322753).

QC links. ADT background comes from unbound antibody in droplets and is measured in empty droplets, so the raw matrix must be kept past cell calling [dsb](../records.md#dsb). snATAC needs its own doublet caller: AMULET counts regions with more than two reads and works best from about 25K median valid reads per nucleus [AMULET](../records.md#amulet). Hashing uses the same antibody-oligo chemistry as CITE-seq and flags cross-sample doublets [Cell Hashing](../records.md#cell-hashing).
