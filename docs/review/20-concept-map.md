# Concept map: how QC choices reach the rest of the analysis

QC is not a step that finishes before the analysis starts. Every choice made at cell calling, ambient removal, thresholding and doublet detection changes what normalisation, integration, clustering, differential expression, perturbation modelling, trajectories and multimodal analyses can see. This map draws those dependencies: the QC categories sit in the middle, the downstream concepts either side, and each line is one effect that a published study has shown or measured.

The concepts themselves are covered at a lighter depth than QC: a definition, the reviews and benchmarks worth reading, and a few anchor tools. Chapters [21](21-concepts-preprocessing.md) and [22](22-concepts-downstream.md) review them.
