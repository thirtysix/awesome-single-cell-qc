# Alignment, quantification, cell calling and ambient RNA

Scope: the steps before any QC threshold is applied, namely how reads become a count matrix, how barcodes become cells, and how ambient RNA is measured and removed. The calling and QC review covers EmptyDrops, CB2, the first three ambient benchmarks and the four main ambient tools in depth ([EmptyDrops](../records.md#emptydrops), [CB2](../records.md#cb2), [CellBender remove-background](../records.md#cellbender), [SoupX](../records.md#soupx), [DecontX](../records.md#decontx), [scAR](../records.md#scar), [janssen-2023](../records.md#janssen-2023), [cargnelli-2026](../records.md#cargnelli-2026), [nie-2026](../records.md#nie-2026)); this chapter adds the quantification layer, the other cell callers, the remaining ambient tools and two 2026 benchmarks, and does not repeat those numbers.

## What the upstream choices do to the cells you keep

The count matrix is not a fixed input. Four choices made before QC change which barcodes look like cells and what they contain.

**The quantifier.** Independent comparisons agree on the direction of the differences. On three 10x datasets, kallisto reported the most cells, but the extra barcodes were low-gene and could not be labelled, and it detected Vmn and Olfr genes that are likely mapping artefacts; salmon alevin, using its own putative whitelist, found only about half of the cardiac endothelial cells that the other four tools found [bruning-2022](../records.md#bruning-2022). On 22 public datasets from eight organisms, Cell Ranger called more cells than kallisto, but outside human and mouse the extras were low quality, while kallisto's higher gene detection revealed an extra photoreceptor type in zebrafish pineal gland [shainer-2021](../records.md#shainer-2021). On mixtures with known labels, emptyDrops called about 60,000 cells on zUMIs and kallisto bustools output for a sample expected to hold about 3,000 [you-2021](../records.md#you-2021). The quantifier and the caller interact: a caller tuned on one pipeline's UMI distribution does not transfer to another's.

**Intronic reads.** Counting reads in introns is now the default in Cell Ranger (from v7.0), and in nuclei it is necessary: exon-only references gave significantly smaller libraries and mislabelled astrocytes as neural stem cells in mouse cortex nuclei [Counting intronic (nascent) reads, especially for nuclei](../records.md#intronic-read-counting). Turning it on shifts every barcode's UMI total, so a knee or a fixed UMI floor from an older run no longer selects the same barcodes.

**The annotation.** Using the unfiltered annotation (with pseudogenes and other biotypes) gave up to 10% more cells per sample and lower mito fractions [bruning-2022](../records.md#bruning-2022). Poor 3' end annotation, intronic handling and overlapping genes each lose reads, and fixing the reference recovered missing marker genes and cell types [Optimised transcriptomic references for scRNA-seq](../records.md#reference-optimisation).

**Ambient RNA.** Every droplet carries some, and nuclei carry more: contamination metrics were universally higher for nuclei than for cells in controlled experiments [arceneaux-2023](../records.md#arceneaux-2023). Uncorrected, ambient transcripts become differentially expressed genes and pathways: in dengue T cells all 17 significant GO terms before correction were driven by immunoglobulin genes [arora-2025](../records.md#arora-2025), and a published brain cell type (immature oligodendrocytes) turned out to be glial nuclei carrying neuronal ambient RNA [caglayan-2022](../records.md#caglayan-2022).

The upshot for salvage: before tuning QC thresholds, make sure the counts and the cell calls are not themselves discarding or inventing cells.

## Standard practice

- **Quantification.** [Cell Ranger](../records.md#cellranger) is the default for 10x data; [STARsolo](../records.md#starsolo) reproduces it much faster and is the engine inside the Human Cell Atlas pipeline [Optimus](../records.md#optimus); [alevin-fry / simpleaf](../records.md#alevin-fry) (via simpleaf) and [kallisto | bustools](../records.md#kallisto-bustools) (via kb-python) are the lightweight alternatives. Plate and custom protocols use [zUMIs](../records.md#zumis), [scPipe](../records.md#scpipe) or [UMI-tools](../records.md#umi-tools). Gene-ambiguous (multi-mapping) reads are discarded by Cell Ranger and by STARsolo unless `--soloMultiMappers` is set; alevin-fry resolves them by EM, and Optimus runs STARsolo with Uniform allocation.
- **Introns.** Count them for nuclei (Cell Ranger include-introns, STARsolo GeneFull, alevin-fry splici or USA mode, kb `nac`). For cells the choice is open; the published comparison found it lowered agreement between workflows without improving clustering [you-2021](../records.md#you-2021).
- **Cell calling.** Pipelines ship a knee or order-of-magnitude rule ([Knee / inflection-point cell calling](../records.md#knee-cell-calling)), with Cell Ranger and STARsolo adding an EmptyDrops-style second step; the limits of that step are in the calling review ([EmptyDrops](../records.md#emptydrops)).
- **Ambient RNA.** SoupX, DecontX or CellBender, as covered in the calling review.

## Benchmarked state of the art

**Quantification.** Five independent comparisons exist. None found a pipeline that changes biological conclusions on well-annotated human or mouse 10x data; the differences sit at the margins that salvage cares about (low-RNA barcodes, lowly expressed genes, non-model organisms).

- You et al. compared 10 workflows on CEL-Seq2 and 10x mixtures with known labels. Almost every combination of workflow, normalisation and clustering recovered the labels; cells unique to a subset of workflows were under 5% of cells. zUMIs and scPipe recovered the most genes; kallisto bustools v0.39.3 was unsuitable for 6-bp UMIs; Cell Ranger was "consistently high and relatively stable" [you-2021](../records.md#you-2021).
- Brüning et al. found STARsolo, Cell Ranger 6, alevin and alevin-fry produced similar gene sets, and STARsolo, alevin and kallisto ran at least three times faster than Cell Ranger 6 [bruning-2022](../records.md#bruning-2022).
- Shainer and Stemmer (above) found kallisto ahead of Cell Ranger on gene detection in non-model organisms, with Cell Ranger calling more but lower-quality cells [shainer-2021](../records.md#shainer-2021).
- Du et al. found STAR detected more genes and agreed better with RNA-FISH than kallisto, at about 4x the time and 7.7x the memory [du-2020](../records.md#du-2020).
- Kuijpers et al. compared eight SPLiT-seq pipelines and recommended Parse's splitpipe or STARsolo, which gave nearly identical results; alevin-fry's splitp mode gave results the authors could not interpret [kuijpers-2024](../records.md#kuijpers-2024).
- In the alevin-fry paper (a competitor comparison, not a benchmark), a spliced-only kallisto|bustools index gave a mean relative false-positive expression of 0.328 per cell against 0.011 for alevin-fry with a splici reference and 0.001 for STARsolo [alevin-fry / simpleaf](../records.md#alevin-fry). The kb `nac` index published since then addresses this but has not been independently re-tested [kallisto | bustools](../records.md#kallisto-bustools).

On this evidence [STARsolo](../records.md#starsolo) is the benchmarked choice when Cell Ranger is too slow or not licensed for the chemistry, [Cell Ranger](../records.md#cellranger) is the stable default, and [kallisto | bustools](../records.md#kallisto-bustools) and [alevin-fry / simpleaf](../records.md#alevin-fry) trade a little specificity at low counts for speed and memory.

**Ambient RNA, beyond the three benchmarks in the calling review.**

- Schroeder et al. (preprint) added a well-plate platform (BD Rhapsody) and a count-integrity test: how often a corrected count exceeds the raw count. CellBender and SoupX removed contamination reliably with minimal distortion. CellClear replaced more than 93% of counts with factorisation-derived values and did not scale to 172,000 nuclei, and scAR created cell types absent from the raw data (three coarse types in BD Rhapsody, up to eight in prefrontal cortex). DecontX and scCDC were the only tools usable without a raw droplet matrix [schroeder-2026](../records.md#schroeder-2026).
- Meier et al. (preprint) tested the order of steps: doublet detection performed best before ambient correction, except with CellBender; running decontX first kept the largest share of doublets, because decontX and SoupX remove proportionally more "ambient" RNA from doublets [meier-2026](../records.md#meier-2026).
- The three tools that the calling review mentioned only in passing now have records. [FastCAR](../records.md#fastcar) tied CellBender for the closest absolute contamination estimate in Nie et al., but tracked per-cell contamination poorly and ranked eighth of nine in Cargnelli et al. [scCDC](../records.md#sccdc) had moderate error and, like FastCAR, corrected mainly highly expressed genes. [CellClear](../records.md#cellclear) had the top subtype-level score in Nie et al. yet removed about as much endogenous as ambient RNA in Cargnelli et al. and rewrote the matrix in Schroeder et al.

Across the five ambient benchmarks, then, CellBender and SoupX are the most consistently recommended, DecontX is the safe choice without a raw matrix, and the aggressive tools (scAR, CellClear) need a count-integrity check before their output is trusted.

**Cell calling.** Still no independent benchmark of cell callers. The comparisons below are each made by one caller's authors.

## Published but not independently benchmarked

**Cell callers.**
- [EmptyNN](../records.md#emptynn) trains a positive-unlabelled neural network. Against hashing ground truth its AUROC was 94.73% against 79.87% for EmptyDrops, 86.88-90.16% for Cell Ranger v2 and v3, and 88.80% for CellBender; against genotype ground truth CellBender kept only 1,686 droplets where the others kept about 6,000 (AUROC 58.02%).
- [dropkick](../records.md#dropkick) fits a per-sample logistic regression to confident cells and empties; specificity 0.9999 on high-background simulations against 0.9838 for EmptyDrops.
- [valiDrops](../records.md#validrops) combines adaptive metric thresholds, a cluster-marker test and a dead-cell classifier (median MCC at least 0.75 with as little as 0.1% dead cells). On a 10x 8k PBMC sample, most callers kept about 8,000 barcodes while dropkick and EmptyNN kept over 9,000.
- [DIEM](../records.md#diem) targets nuclei: across six adipose samples 95.7% of its passing droplets were nuclear, against 87.9% for EmptyDrops and 84.5% for a quantile threshold, and EmptyDrops removed 1,038 and 782 nuclear droplets in two samples where DIEM removed 63 and 36.
- [SiftCell](../records.md#siftcell) contrasts each barcode with shuffled counterparts; no numbers in its abstract.
- QCatch ([QCatch](../records.md#qcatch)) adds cell calling and QC reports to alevin-fry output.
- Read-level callers for pooled or combinatorial designs: [ambimux](../records.md#ambimux) estimates per-droplet ambient fractions while demultiplexing genotype-pooled multiome data and kept singlets correctly up to 60% ambient, against about 25% for ambient-unaware models; [AmbientMapper](../records.md#ambientmapper) does the same with multiple references for scifi-ATAC and rescues low-depth nuclei.

**Ambient tools.** [AmbiDose](../records.md#ambidose) (preprint) caps removal per cell and protects lineage genes; on a held-out human-mouse mixture it removed 92.0% of cross-species UMIs while keeping 99.6% of same-species UMIs. [CellBouncer](../records.md#cellbouncer) estimates, in pooled experiments, each donor's or species' contribution to the ambient pool. [CLEANSER](../records.md#cleanser) applies the same idea to CRISPR guides: a mixture model built on barnyard assays gives each gRNA-cell assignment a probability of being native rather than ambient, and its authors report that the choice of ambient filter changed differential expression results. [Spike-in reference cells](../records.md#spike-in-reference-cells) and [DecontPro](../records.md#decontpro) (tag counts) are covered elsewhere.

**Quantification.** [cellCounts](../records.md#cellcounts) brings 10x quantification into R (Rsubread); [UMI-tools](../records.md#umi-tools) remains the UMI deduplication engine of many custom pipelines; [Optimised transcriptomic references for scRNA-seq](../records.md#reference-optimisation) rebuilds the annotation for droplet 3' data. Lightweight-mapping comparisons by the kallisto and alevin-fry authors (bioRxiv 10.1101/2021.01.25.428188 and 10.1101/2021.02.10.430656) are competitor comparisons and are not counted as benchmarks.

**Wet-lab ambient reduction.** In controlled inDrop experiments, cold protease dissociation, 0.1X DSP fixation, direct tip loading into the chip and about 1:1 dilution all lowered contamination metrics; 1% PFA fixation failed QC [arceneaux-2023](../records.md#arceneaux-2023). See [DSP reversible fixation](../records.md#dsp-fixation) for fixation in screen designs.

## Nuclei, intronic reads and multimapping: signal or artefact

The calling review asks whether a barcode is damaged or just different. Upstream, the same question applies to reads.

- *Intronic reads in nuclei are signal.* Most nuclear RNA is unspliced; dropping it shrinks libraries and blurs related types [Counting intronic (nascent) reads, especially for nuclei](../records.md#intronic-read-counting). They also carry a gene-length bias, plausibly from internal priming in long introns, which matters when comparing genes, not cells (PMID 37257175).
- *Intronic reads in cells are mostly noise for clustering* [you-2021](../records.md#you-2021) but are what velocity and damage detection use: [DropletQC](../records.md#dropletqc) calls damage from the unspliced fraction, so a pipeline that drops intronic reads removes the evidence the damage caller needs. Different tools split spliced, unspliced and ambiguous reads differently (distinguishing flanking k-mers versus splici and spliceu references; PMID 39657125), and that choice changes velocity results [soneson-2021-velocity-preprocessing](../records.md#soneson-2021-velocity-preprocessing).
- *Multimapping reads are signal for some gene families.* Discarding gene-ambiguous reads biases against paralogues and genes with low mappability; STARsolo and alevin-fry recover them by EM or uniform allocation [STARsolo](../records.md#starsolo), [alevin-fry / simpleaf](../records.md#alevin-fry). Pseudoalignment against a spliced-only index goes the other way and creates false-positive genes [kallisto | bustools](../records.md#kallisto-bustools).
- *Ambient is higher in nuclei*, and in high-ambient nuclei data a count threshold keeps debris that a profile-based caller removes [DIEM](../records.md#diem), [Knee / inflection-point cell calling](../records.md#knee-cell-calling).

## Hypotheses and open questions

- No independent benchmark of cell callers exists. The four caller papers above disagree on whether EmptyDrops, CellBender or their own method is best, and on the same 8k PBMC sample callers differed by about 1,000 barcodes. A ground-truth resource (hashing or genotype labels on empty and cell-containing droplets, as EmptyNN used) scored across callers would settle it.
- Callers are tuned to one quantifier's UMI distribution; the 60,000-cell emptyDrops call on zUMIs and kallisto output [you-2021](../records.md#you-2021) suggests that switching quantifier, or switching intronic counting on, silently changes calling. Not measured systematically.
- Order of steps: if Meier et al. hold up, pipelines that correct ambient before calling doublets (with decontX or SoupX) are losing doublet calls [meier-2026](../records.md#meier-2026). Only a preprint so far.

## Recommendations for low-cell multiplexed drug screens

Ranked by expected gain per unit of effort. Gains are from the cited settings, not from drug screens.

1. **Keep the raw matrix and the BAM.** Every caller and the better ambient tools need the raw matrix, and damage and ambient demultiplexing need reads. *Gain:* keeps every later rescue possible. *Data:* raw-matrix, bam.
2. **Fix quantification before thresholds.** Use [Cell Ranger](../records.md#cellranger) or [STARsolo](../records.md#starsolo) (Cell Ranger-like output, faster); count introns for nuclei [Counting intronic (nascent) reads, especially for nuclei](../records.md#intronic-read-counting); enable multi-gene allocation if paralogous response genes matter. Use the same pipeline and version for every lane. *Gain:* up to 10% more cells per sample from the annotation alone [bruning-2022](../records.md#bruning-2022). *Data:* fastq.
3. **Re-call cells with a profile-aware caller, not a knee.** emptyDrops with a low floor, then check rescued barcodes with nuclear fraction and tag strength (calling review); for nuclei consider [DIEM](../records.md#diem); treat [EmptyNN](../records.md#emptynn), [dropkick](../records.md#dropkick) and [valiDrops](../records.md#validrops) as second opinions, since none is independently benchmarked. *Gain:* self-reported only. *Data:* raw-matrix, tag-counts.
4. **Call doublets before ambient correction, then correct per lane.** [CellBender remove-background](../records.md#cellbender) (order-independent), or [SoupX](../records.md#soupx) or [DecontX](../records.md#decontx) after doublet calling [meier-2026](../records.md#meier-2026). Avoid tools that rewrite the matrix ([scAR](../records.md#scar), [CellClear](../records.md#cellclear)) unless a count-integrity check passes [schroeder-2026](../records.md#schroeder-2026). *Gain:* spurious ambient DEGs and pathways removed in [arora-2025](../records.md#arora-2025); doublets retained otherwise. *Data:* raw-matrix.
5. **At the bench:** cold dissociation, reversible fixation and dilution lower ambient at the source [arceneaux-2023](../records.md#arceneaux-2023); superloading with tags raises cells per lane ([Superloading with sample multiplexing](../records.md#superloading)); its effect on ambient load has not been measured.
